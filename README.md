**Legendary Sword** is set after the sacrifice of the "Seven Stars of Heroes," in a world 100 years later ravaged by brutal monsters and war, players will excavate ruins to uncover the truth, defeat bosses, and revive the kingdom.
## Features 
Legendary Sword aims to expand player's gaming experience, we currently have the following features.
+ **Powerful Weapons**: Addon have added 6 swords and 1 spear, with the most powerful one outperforms netherite in durability and damage.
+ **Various Trinkets**: We now have 4 trinkets available, whose functions range from giving buffs to applying special benifits, such as tripling arrows shooted.
+ **Comprehensive Guiding System**: We've added following mechanism to guide you into game.
	+ **Tasks**: We have 13 tasks in 3 chapters. Moreover, an extra easter egg task is provided.
	+ **Articles**: Articles showcases the world outlook of this addon, which can be found in relics in the world.
+ **Easy Starting**: We provide ways to improve your task history from the following addons to simplify your experience.
	+ [Hidden Years²](https://hy.ctnstudios.top)
